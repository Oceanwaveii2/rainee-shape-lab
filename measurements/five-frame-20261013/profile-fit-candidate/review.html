Method and uncertainty

Review-guided outer arcs are sampled every ~3 px. A blurred-Lab derivative searches ±8 px along curve normals. Soft-L1 rotated ellipse/circle fits use outside arcs only. Partial-arc fits can have low curve error yet highly unstable hidden centers/radii; those are diagnostics. The primary ellipse uses exact polygon area/centroid/second moments and preserves the visible polygon area.

The denominator is a manually annotated visible exposed lower-back skin span at image row620, excluding sleeves. It is garment/pose dependent. ±8 px endpoint tolerance is shown in JSON, not a claim of anatomical measurement accuracy. Shape sensitivity uses correlated ±3 px outside-curve jitter and ±5 px clothing/cleft closure jitter; it excludes pose and generation uncertainty.

For camera fitting, use the observed outside curves and supports first. Downweight the occluded seams and clipped lower poles. Do not turn ellipse/pixel area or stage labels into physical volume multipliers.
