Rainee: ideas for a program that measures and constructs her shape
Research synthesis, 2026-10-14

This is a proposal, not an implemented or validated reconstruction system.
The character build remains paused while the user prepares more data.
The paper descriptions below are research findings. Their proposed uses for
Rainee are our engineering inferences, not results demonstrated on her assets.

Main direction
--------------
Give the program three responsibilities: understand the evidence, construct an
editable canonical body, and explain where a proposed shape disagrees with the
evidence. Preserve alternative plausible shapes when a view cannot resolve
depth. A new representation improves authoring, but does not supply missing
side views, trustworthy camera parameters, or structural attachment information.

The accepted sequence remains 0000 -> 0010 -> 0018 -> 0026, then hold 0026.
These are four changing shape states, not four ordinary views of one static
object. Stage ordinals do not specify anatomical volume ratios. The FBX supplies
the existing stature anchor; accepted images guide the face. Thighs remain
separately authored. The initial native control-volume schedule is provisional.

1. Separate the variables that currently get confused
---------------------------------------------------
Each reference should have its own camera, pose, shape stage and appearance.
One canonical body supplies identity and measurements. A local growth field
supplies the rear shape. Pose supplies lumbar arch, pelvis orientation and
limb placement. The camera supplies perspective, framing and apparent size.
Appearance supplies highlights, costume and painted surface detail.

Fit broad silhouettes and reviewed landmarks first, then selected surface
profiles, then detail. Starting with all RGB pixels would encourage the solver
to interpret glossy reflections, clothing folds and generated shading as anatomy.

BARF (ICCV 2021) demonstrates coarse-to-fine representation/camera alignment.
Its camera-alignment idea is useful, but our changing, independently generated
references do not satisfy an ordinary static-scene reconstruction assumption.
https://chenhsuanlin.bitbucket.io/bundle-adjusting-NeRF/

Large Steps (SIGGRAPH Asia 2021) demonstrates a more stable parameterization for
image-driven geometry optimization. Proposed use: optimize a coarse regional
shape against registered outlines while protecting stature and distant anatomy.
Its demonstrations do not establish the correct hidden anatomy in our artwork.
https://rgl.epfl.ch/publications/Nicolet2021Large

2. Give the character a small, meaningful shape vocabulary
---------------------------------------------------------
Proposed controls: lateral width, posterior depth, vertical fullness, upper
dome, underside fullness, left/right spacing, cleft profile, and attachment
width. Keep lumbar arch and pelvic tilt in the pose controls. Keep thigh mass,
front/outer sweep and knee taper in their own controls.

Begin with a connected pelvic template and two lobe fields. Add local profile
corrections when simple ellipsoids cannot match the curves. Expose artist terms;
the numerical implementation can use implicit functions, cages and coefficients.
Report individual lobe quantities and their combined region consistently.
Adding the volumes of overlapping primitives would double-count their overlap.

Semantic Shape Editing with Parametric Implicit Templates (SIGGRAPH 2024) fits
an analytical implicit template to a mesh and tracks its surface as template
parameters change, including anisotropic stretch and local volume changes.
This is a particularly direct foundation for a custom anatomy authoring tool.
Our pelvic/lobe/thigh template would still need deliberate fitting and joins;
a generic smooth union can erase the cleft or overblend the thigh transition.
https://udaykusupati.github.io/publications/ish/ish/

3. Explore curved cages and incremental deformation fields
---------------------------------------------------------
Bézier Green cages (SIGGRAPH 2025) use curved boundaries and position/normal
controls. Proposed use: manipulate round poles with a few handles, preserving
the broader body shape. Numerical integration and preprocessing add cost, and
the method is not an automatic guarantee against folds or contact intersections.
https://github.com/Submanifold/BezierGreen
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.14068

Vector-field deformation (SIGGRAPH 2006) integrates a field over time rather
than applying one large displacement. Its original divergence-free construction
preserves volume. Proposed adaptation: separate deliberate growth from shaping,
and integrate controlled increments while pinning distant anatomy. Numerical
integration accuracy, field regularity and collision checks remain necessary.
https://vc.cs.ovgu.de/assets/publications/2006/Funck_2006_SIGGRAPH.pdf

4. Use an implicit surface for structure and surface extraction
--------------------------------------------------------------
A signed distance function can support connected geometry and local blending
without depending on the inherited vertex layout. A small local grid or sparse
field around pelvis and rear could provide a workable first experiment. Keep
the rest of the character on an editable mesh/cage while fitting that region.

Differentiable SDF Rendering (SIGGRAPH 2022) supplies image-based optimization
of implicit geometry and visibility. FlexiCubes (SIGGRAPH 2023) supplies flexible,
differentiable surface extraction with optional adaptive/tetrahedral extensions.
Proposed use: optimize the regional field and extract a usable surface, then
inspect continuity, creases and mesh quality before production retopology.
Neither paper makes an invented hidden attachment anatomically authoritative.
https://rgl.epfl.ch/publications/Vicini2022SDF
https://research.nvidia.com/labs/toronto-ai/flexicubes/

OpenVDB provides a sparse level-set alternative already relevant to Houdini.
Fine voxels can consume memory and smooth away creases; remeshing changes
correspondence. Use sufficient resolution where the attachment changes rapidly,
and keep construction operations reversible.
https://www.openvdb.org/documentation/

5. Test a compact surface-patch representation
--------------------------------------------
MASH (SIGGRAPH 2025) represents shapes with anchored spherical-distance patches,
harmonic coefficients and local masks. Its deterministic fitting branch is
separate from its generation branch. Proposed use: fit a few local charts to
rear surfaces and expose selected coefficients as curvature controls. That may
capture richer shape than two ellipsoids without editing every vertex.
Patch initialization, continuity and watertight extraction need attention;
geometry fitting does not automatically resolve appearance or anatomy.
https://565353780.github.io/MASH/
https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09149v3

6. Give Gaussians an appearance role with a controlled deformation
----------------------------------------------------------------
The existing SPZs are useful visual assets. A shared deformation can move their
centres and transport covariance alongside a geometric control surface. A
segmented character region is essential: the room, glass, clothing and hair
must have explicitly chosen roles. Gaussian opacity/covariance is not tissue
density, and summing Gaussian ellipsoid volumes does not measure the body.

SuGaR (CVPR 2024) demonstrates mesh extraction and surface-bound Gaussians.
PhysGaussian (CVPR 2024) couples Gaussian appearance with continuum deformation.
Proposed use: retain appearance while the authored surface/field defines shape.
For a nonlinear deformation, J Sigma J^T is first-order covariance transport;
it is exact for the local affine approximation, not a whole finite Gaussian.
https://imagine.enpc.fr/~guedona/sugar/
https://xpandora.github.io/PhysGaussian/

2D Gaussian Splatting (SIGGRAPH 2024) uses oriented surface disks and geometry
regularization. Existing 3D SPZ exports do not become this representation merely
by being opened in a viewer. Reconstruction/refinement still requires suitable
images and camera information.
https://surfsplatting.github.io/

Gaussian Sculpting (August 2026 arXiv preprint) makes an evolving SDF surface
the geometric authority and constrains Gaussians to it. This is a useful hybrid
architecture to study. It is a costly research branch: its paper reports 6–18
hours per scene on a 24 GB RTX 3090 and discusses memory and reflective-material
limitations. That is the paper's benchmark, not a local performance prediction.
No SIGGRAPH acceptance or installation is claimed here.
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10602v1

Drag Your Gaussian (SIGGRAPH 2025) offers masked point-based edits with score
distillation and a latent diffusion model. Its interaction ideas are relevant,
but the full method adds a generative prior. Generated changes would need
explicit provenance and artist review; they are not recovered measurements.
https://quyans.github.io/Drag-Your-Gaussian/

7. Preserve uncertainty and choose the next useful view
------------------------------------------------------
The program should distinguish observed outlines, artist-defined shape, and
unseen/ambiguous regions. For an underconstrained depth, show multiple plausible
solutions with comparable rear-view error. Report ranges produced by those
solutions rather than a falsely precise circumference or volume. Those ranges
are sensitivity results, not calibrated probability intervals automatically.

Uncertainty-Aware Geometry Processing on Gaussian Process Implicit Surfaces
(SIGGRAPH 2026) performs geometric computation on probabilistic surfaces with
noise/missing-data uncertainty. This suggests a useful architectural direction.
Gaussian-process surfaces here are distinct from rendering Gaussian splats.
A valid probabilistic model and uncertainty assumptions must be supplied.
https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/david.coeurjolly/publication/gpgp-26/

Our proposed next-view tool: render several acceptable candidate shapes from
possible viewpoints, identify where their outlines diverge most, and recommend
that view for the next artist-approved reference. A simple parameter-sensitivity
version could precede a full probabilistic implementation.

8. Turn generated donor models into regional evidence
-----------------------------------------------------
Put every generated model in a reviewed anatomical coordinate frame. Mark
pelvis, waist, lobe peaks, thigh roots and knees. Register its selected body
region onto the canonical template before comparing deformation. Exclude props,
costume and melted/disconnected regions where appropriate. Keep donor source,
registration error and artist acceptance attached to every derived control.

Deformation Transfer (SIGGRAPH 2004) demonstrates transfer across different
mesh vertex counts/connectivity with a correspondence map. Proposed use:
compare registered regional fields rather than subtracting unrelated vertices.
Independent generated models require semantic registration first; that process
does not make every donor defect part of the intended body.
https://people.csail.mit.edu/sumner/research/deftransfer/

9. Author growth, then add motion and contact
--------------------------------------------
First fit the four intended shapes and a smooth progression that holds the
endpoint. Then treat that progression as changing rest geometry. Elasticity,
damping and contact can produce settling or secondary motion around that target.
Pressure alone is an unnecessarily indirect way to reproduce the drawings.

Projective Dynamics (SIGGRAPH 2014) and XPBD (Motion in Games 2016) provide useful
elastic/compliant simulation foundations. Houdini Vellum Rest Blend accepts
matching-topology rest geometry. Incremental Potential Contact (SIGGRAPH 2020)
provides a stronger but more demanding contact/inversion-avoidance direction.
All need suitable solids/surfaces, attachments and material choices.
https://users.cs.utah.edu/~ladislav/bouaziz14projective/bouaziz14projective.html
https://mmacklin.com/xpbd.pdf
https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/nodes/dop/vellumrestblend.html
https://ipc-sim.github.io/

Volume conservation during ordinary motion and an intentional increase in the
authored shape's volume are separate requirements. A simulation should settle
around the currently authored state rather than undo the accepted endpoint.

Program features proposed for exploration
----------------------------------------
- Paired 2D reference and 3D views with contour mismatch overlays.
- Rear and side profile editing plus cross-sections at selected heights.
- A dimension ledger in FBX-relative units, with camera/pose-independent
  definitions, closed-region definitions and observed/artist/unknown status.
- A pelvis coordinate frame that stays stable across donors and shape stages.
- Separate rear, thighs, posture, face, appearance and secondary-motion controls.
- A source-linked library of regional donor shape cues with reviewed masks.
- Depth alternatives and a next-view recommendation based on disagreement.
- A simple neutral clay preview to judge geometry separately from highlights.
- Measurement sensitivity when camera estimates, masks or landmarks change.
- A coarse interactive preview with local refinement after the large shape fits.

Small experiments to run after the build resumes
-----------------------------------------------
A. Make a simple synthetic character with known dimensions. Render changing
camera, pose and growth independently. Check whether the fitter recovers the
known quantities, or correctly reports ambiguity when the side view is absent.
B. Fit low-dimensional lobe controls to rear + side profiles. Reserve a rear
three-quarter view for validation rather than optimizing every supplied view.
C. Compare an implicit template, curved cage and local grid on the same input,
using silhouette error, structural continuity, field orientation, edit latency
and peak memory. Keep stature and unaffected anatomy fixed in every experiment.
D. Test clipping and garment occlusion. Verify that an unseen lower pole remains
unknown instead of being promoted from an extrapolated ellipse into a fact.
E. After accepting shape, compare simple elastic secondary motion with the
authored rest target held. Audit contact and output shape separately.

Priority for this project
-------------------------
Start with camera/pose separation, semantic dimensions and editable implicit
templates. Add stable contour fitting and confidence/sensitivity displays.
Evaluate curved cages and local SDF/FlexiCubes on a small regional experiment.
Keep the supplied Gaussians available for appearance inspection and controlled
deformation. MASH is a promising compact-shape experiment. Full neural surface
training and expensive contact solvers can follow only when their added value
has been demonstrated on the bounded tests.

The important advance is a coherent, inspectable parameter space with a
reliable comparison loop. The program should make extreme stylization an
explicit authored property while retaining the evidence and uncertainty behind
every measurement.
