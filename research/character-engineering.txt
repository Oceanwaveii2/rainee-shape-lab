WHAT WAS LEARNED FROM THE REFERENCES
Grok's rear lobes read as near-round caps: domed upper poles, rounded lower
poles, narrow medial roots, and substantial overhang beyond the underlying hips.
Approximate image aspect 0.9-1.1 width/height is a qualitative guide, not a 3D
measurement. There is no clean orthogonal side view in that clip.
Thighs carry a long upper-to-middle convex sweep and taper into narrower knees.
They have a separate authoring control; butt inflation does not auto-enlarge them.

ART-DIRECTED DEFORMATION REFERENCES
Pose Space Deformation, SIGGRAPH 2000, Lewis/Cordner/Fong:
https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~jacobson/seminar/lewis-et-al-2000.pdf
Useful principle: deformation is an authored function of shape/pose parameters,
with corrective examples. Rainee currently uses custom procedural mappings,
not a complete PSD implementation or an inferred anatomical model.

Bounded Biharmonic Weights, SIGGRAPH 2011, Jacobson et al.:
https://igl.ethz.ch/projects/bbw/
Useful principle: local handles and smooth shape-aware influence weights, with
binding work precomputed. Candidate replacement for hand-coded coordinate masks;
BBW weight optimization has not been implemented in this project.

Implicit Skinning, SIGGRAPH 2013, Vaillant et al.:
https://www.rodolphe-vaillant.fr/entry/31/implicit-skinning-real-time-skin-deformation-with-contact-modeli
Useful principle: volumetric implicit representations can refine skin contact and
organic bulges after geometric skinning. Candidate for lobe/thigh contact, not
implemented or certified by current positive field-Jacobian checks.

IMAGE RECONSTRUCTION / REPRESENTATION REFERENCES
NeRF, ECCV 2020:
https://www.matthewtancik.com/nerf
3D Gaussian Splatting, SIGGRAPH 2023:
https://repo-sam.inria.fr/fungraph/3d-gaussian-splatting/
SC-GS, CVPR 2024:
https://yihua7.github.io/SC-GS-web/
These are useful for appearance and view synthesis; SC-GS demonstrates sparse
control editing of dynamic Gaussians. Gaussian/radiance density is not a closed
collision body or a measured anatomical solid. No NeRF/3DGS training was run.

NeuS, NeurIPS 2021:
https://lingjie0206.github.io/papers/NeuS/index.htm
DMTet, NeurIPS 2021:
https://research.nvidia.com/labs/toronto-ai/DMTet/
These provide surface-centered SDF/tetrahedral approaches. They are better
directions for rebuilding a continuous shape than appearance-only density.
Regenerating topology requires correspondence/UV/binding work. Neither model
has been trained or installed here.

nvdiffrast, TOG 2020:
https://nvlabs.github.io/nvdiffrast/
Provides differentiable rasterization primitives. A silhouette fitter would
still need camera fitting, pose/state selection, priors, and a deformation model.
Conflicting generated references are not calibrated multiview capture data.

Houdini VDB from Polygons, official Houdini 22 documentation:
https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/nodes/sop/vdbfrompolygons.html
Sparse SDF volumes are a practical local modeling option. Unsigned distance
does not require an airtight source; true interior/pressure assumptions do.

CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION / LIMITS
v1: coarse regional scaling with measured closed-cage volume and native Vellum.
v2: independently authored thigh sculpt, neutral at zero strength.
v3: paired lobe center/radius growth, explicit upper/lower poles and cleft hold,
plus a point-sampled field determinant guard. This improves cage proportions,
but the source body transfer still needs visual pole-shape refinement.
Positive sampled determinants do not prove global injectivity or triangle contact.
Unsafe settings (including direct default 4x growth) are rejected rather than
treated as valid merely because closed-cage volume is numerically correct.

rear-flow-prototype.py explores RK4 integration of a stationary growth velocity.
Sampled 2x/3x/4x and a difficult direction case avoid the direct-map folds.
Full-body NumPy cost is about 1.3-1.8 seconds per 98k-point evaluation at 24 steps.
This remains research; no claim of native integration, full-range correctness,
physical contact, or completed artistic match is made.

NEXT RECONSTRUCTION ARCHITECTURE (PROJECT DESIGN INFERENCE)
1. Repair the neutral pelvic connection as a joined external surface.
2. Fit per-reference cameras/poses to stature and protected body landmarks.
3. Fit selected silhouettes with separate rear lobe and thigh shape controls.
4. Use procedural SDF/voxel surface correction where the current body cannot
   express the rounded poles or folds; retain a stable editable deformation layer.
5. Add secondary motion and contact after the authored target shape is accepted.


CURRENT V4 AND NATIVE ROUNDING ADDENDUM
The local VDB pelvic repair is now integrated in Rainee-Volume-Lab-v4.hip.
Eight native rear/thigh combinations retain FBX stature. The repaired core is
connected; inherited topology defects and outer collar cleanup remain.

The separate rear-rounding/native-study/Rainee-Optional-Radial-Rounding.hip
compiles radial relaxation in a native VFL node. Four static states (1, 1.5,
2, 2.5x closed-cage volume) pass all sampled rear-region determinant checks.
This differs from the earlier stationary-growth prototype mentioned above.
The browser rounds through these actual native caches; arbitrary rounded
settings, dynamic contact, and exact video fidelity remain unverified.

Both native studies include an export branch that removes unused points and
restores the referenced surface to 2.3951061964 FBX source units. Source units
do not establish centimetres. Normalized internal solver coordinates remain.

Supplied generated 3D figurines now feed a local measurement pipeline. Texture
role candidates and explicit surface ROIs exclude most hair, stands and props.
Dimensionless measurements are provisional shape cues; generated pose and
region differences do not constitute a calibrated scan or anatomical volume.
See ../measurements/inspection.html and ../measurements/README.txt.
